# Stimma 1.0.18: Fluent prompting, Contact Sheets, MiniMax H3 Image, and major workspace upgrades

Thank you to everyone using Stimma and sharing feedback. This release introduces **Fluent** for controlled prompt variation, **Contact Sheets** for reviewing agent work cleanly, local **MiniMax H3 Image** generation and editing, steadier long-form video with **MiniMax H3**, **Quick Ask**, background agent work, cross-device sync, and a cleaner workspace throughout.

## Fluent: controlled variety without the busywork

Modern image models tend to repeat themselves across seeds. Rewriting every prompt with an LLM adds variety, but quickly falls into repetitive "AI-slop" phrasing. Traditional wildcards handle one or two swaps fine, but stack several together and random draws start contradicting each other.

**Fluent** is a prompting engine unique to Stimma that automates the work of writing rich variations while keeping you in control of what stays fixed and what changes. It runs ahead of Stimma's existing prompt pipeline, so `[exact text]`, `{a|b|c}` inline wildcards, `{{name}}` saved lists and prompt segments, and `# comment` lines all still work the same way.

Wrap any part of your prompt in `<angle brackets>` using a description or example:

- **Prompt-aware option pools:** Your Tool Screen model reads each phrase in context and writes coherent options in the background. Because it builds a pool instead of rewriting every image from scratch, you get real variety without repetitive LLM habits.
- **Full control over every pool:** Open any phrase beneath the prompt to search, pin a subset, delete misses, add your own entries, or generate **More**.
- **Add Detail vs. Add Variety:** Go deeper or wider on any phrase. Wrap `<a knife>` and **Add Detail** describes what that knife looks like; **Add Variety** widens it to other objects. Suggestions can also highlight phrases worth fleshing out without rewriting your prompt.
- **Use in your prompt:** Pull any option Fluent picked straight from a generated image back into your prompt.
- **Automatic conflict checking on your CPU:** Fluent avoids contradictions at three layers: prompt-aware option writing, vocabulary filtering, and a fine-tuned 435M **DeBERTa-v3-large** judge running locally on your CPU. It catches blatant clashes across multiple varying parts and rerolls them automatically, cutting conflicting prompt outputs down to single-digit percentages, mostly in gray areas. It downloads in the background while your Tool Screen model stands in, so generation never blocks.
- **Upgraded prompt improver & Prompting menu:** Stimma's prompt improver is upgraded across the board while preserving model-specific formats like Ideogram and MiniMax H3. Switch between **Fluent**, **Direct** for the familiar pre-Fluent pipeline, and **Raw** from one **Prompting** menu, and inspect **Original** → **Filled in** → **Improved** / **Sent** in the Info Panel and Share pages.

Fluent requires a configured **Tool Screen** model in Settings. It was benchmarked primarily on **Qwen 3.8 27B**, **Claude Haiku 5.5**, and **GPT-6 Luna** — fast models in that class are the sweet spot.

## Contact Sheets: review agent work without digging through long chats

In long agent sessions, tracking which outputs are finished work and which were intermediate tests gets exhausting. **Contact Sheets** give the agent a structured way to deliver finished results beside the conversation so you can review and iterate cleanly:

- Browse uncropped grids, columns, collapsible sections, and before/after **Compare** sliders right next to chat.
- Rate images with thumbs or stars, apply labels, leave per-image or section notes, and answer structured choices inline — then send your entire review back in one turn.
- Powered by the new **Contact Sheets** skill in **Stimma Essentials**.

## Local MiniMax H3 Image in ComfyUI

**MiniMax H3 Image** brings high-resolution open-weights generation and multi-reference editing to ComfyUI-Stimma, paired with dedicated H3 Image prompt enhancement in Stimma:

- **Generation & editing in one workflow:** Text-to-image and multi-reference editing with up to **16 ordered references** (`<Picture 1>`, `<Picture 2>`, …), defaulting to **4096×4096**, with LoRA slots and Advanced sampling controls.
- **Image Editor support:** **MiniMax H3 Image Inpaint** powers **Repaint** and **Remove** while preserving pixels outside the mask exactly. **MiniMax H3 Image Outpaint** joins **Expand** as an Experimental option.
- **Dedicated prompt enhancement:** Stimma enhances H3 Image prompts through your configured chat model using image-specific generation and edit guides — no separate local expander model needed.

Update both Stimma and ComfyUI-Stimma, install the models in the provider manager, and restart ComfyUI. **MiniMax H3 Image requires ComfyUI v0.45.3 or newer** — updating the connector does not update ComfyUI itself. Due to its community license, H3 Image is available locally through ComfyUI-Stimma rather than on Stimma Account Credits.

## More reliable long-form video with MiniMax H3 and seamkit

Making longer continuous videos with open models takes more than raw generation — it means keeping motion steady, avoiding surprise mid-clip cuts, and joining clips cleanly in both picture and sound:

- **Sound-only timeline keyframes:** Pin an audio track — or a clip's sound without its video — across a timeline span in local MiniMax H3 workflows so H3 follows and speaks your audio while stills or clips guide the visuals.
- **Fewer surprise cuts:** Stimma's H3 prompt rewriter treats pinned start frames as owning the opening shot, describing what changes instead of restating the frame into an unintended cut, and avoids writing dialogue over pinned speech.
- **State-preserving continuations:** MiniMax H3 preserves original generation state across overlapping continuations, with an Info Panel warning if stored state goes unused.
- **Smarter `seamkit` checks and joins:** The updated **Video Production** skill in **Stimma Essentials** checks for hard cuts, tracks visual drift, and verifies anchors before rendering. Its joiner trims slowdowns around pinned stills, repairs repeated-frame judder, matches exposure and color, switches audio at quiet moments or locks a single master track from frame 0, and records clip lineage automatically.

## Quick Ask, Chat Dock, and background agent work

- **Quick Ask & Chat Dock:** Right-click any media or selection and type right into the top of the menu to open a resizable floating chat window that follows you across the app.
- **Background work & live peek:** Keep chatting while agent scripts and batches run. Click the progress bar above the composer to peek at finished, in-flight, and failed items live, or stop remaining work without losing completed results.
- **Longer autonomous runs:** Agents can now run up to **1,000 steps** with check-in timers, editable memory under **Settings > Agent > Memory**, and **Always allow \<provider\>** for local providers.
- **Better context awareness:** Agents see your marks, collections, and edits on chat media, and code-generated outputs preserve source lineage.
- **Auto-syncing Stimpacks:** Built-in marketplace stimpacks now stay up to date automatically, with a **Reset** option in **Settings > Stimpacks** if you edit a built-in skill.

## Redesigned workspace, Collections, and LoRA notes

- **Cleaner workspace:** Refined contrast, unified headers and rows, sentence-case labels, and streamlined floating panels in Tool View.
- **Model-family Tools catalog:** Tool variants group as chips under model families, provider badges appear wherever a tool is named, and **All Tools** adds **Media**, **Task**, and **Provider** filters.
- **Boards are now Collections:** Renamed across the app and docs, with markers shown on collection tiles.
- **Instant `/` prompt search & single-line filters:** Press **`/`** to filter assets live by prompt or title. Active filter chips now tuck into the expandable **Filters** panel.
- **Faster media swapping:** Filled input slots add a **Replace** button, dragging media over a sidebar tool tab pops out per-slot drop targets, and the wider Library Picker adds **Used before**, **All**, **Generated**, and **Imported** tabs with prompt search.
- **LoRA Trigger Words & Notes:** Edit **Trigger words** and **Notes for the AI** inline on any LoRA and share a note across a model series. ComfyUI-Stimma fingerprints LoRA files so notes survive renames and folder moves.
- **Slideshow & viewer improvements:** Focus mode auto-hides controls after 3 seconds idle and keeps your screen awake. Press **`Enter`** to open slideshows anywhere and **`Backspace`** to trash, open Artifact Panel media straight into the slideshow, play chat videos inline, and hover any chat message for its timestamp.
- **Diagnostics & experiments:** Export scrubbed diagnostic logs via **Help > Export Logs…** on macOS and Windows. **Flows** is now an opt-in experiment under **Settings > Preferences > Enable Flows**.

## Cross-device sync, faster media, and model updates

- **Cross-device workspace sync:** Open and pinned tabs, tool settings, prompts, LoRA pools, drafts, and Chat Dock state sync between desktop and phone in about a second, while local UI preferences stay per device.
- **Faster browsing & remote video:** High-resolution images load fast screen-sized previews and fetch full originals on zoom. Long videos start faster over slow or [remote connections](https://docs.stimma.ai/remote-access/).
- **New cloud models & local LLM improvements:** Added **GPT-6.2 Astra**, **GPT-6.2 Sol**, **GPT-6.1 Terra**, **Claude Sonnet 5.5**, **Claude Haiku 5.5**, and **Claude Fable 5.2** (on Stimma Account Credits, plus OpenAI and ChatGPT accounts for GPT-6.2), along with dedicated **Krea 2** prompt enhancement. The chat model picker deduplicates onto your best working account, and swapping models on a local LLM server offers a one-step **Switch to \<model\>** button.
- **Multi-host ComfyUI fix:** ComfyUI-Stimma now routes progress and results only to the Stimma host that submitted the job.

## In development: the mobile interface

Work continues on the iOS and Android apps with native **Print**, **Save to device**, continuous native **Voice input**, swipeable Tool View results, a dedicated **Forever** button beside **Run**, and a compact floating Chat Dock button.

**The mobile apps are not released and are not available in the App Store or Google Play.** Developers can build them from [source](https://stimma.ai/source/). Your connected computer or server still runs generation.

## Reliability and fixes

- Much faster headless server updates on large libraries, with prompt restarts and DNS rebinding protection.
- Fixed database lock collisions between agent runs and finishing jobs, crashes when emptying very large trashes, and added safer migrations with automatic rollback.
- Workers recover promptly after silent providers, cancellations, or restarts, and Generate Forever no longer stalls during long prompt prep.
- Fixed Image Editor undo/redo, stale overwrites, SVG exports, image orientation, rotated video playback, and mask cleanup on startup.
- Fixed Tool View's agent overwriting new prompts after Remix / Use Prompt, kept sent steers out of drafts, stopped hidden chat videos from playing, and fixed stimpacks appearing to need an update on every launch.
- Updated dependencies for security.
